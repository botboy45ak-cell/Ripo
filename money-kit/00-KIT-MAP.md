## The business in one paragraph

Local businesses in Patna (cafés, salons, gyms, coaching centres, boutiques, doctors, shops) mostly have a phone but no website. They don't know how to get one, and agencies quote them ₹15,000–₹50,000. You sell a one-page website for ₹999–₹3,499, built from a template that already works, delivered in 24–48 hours. Each client takes you **1–2 hours of actual work**. Ten clients a month at an average ₹1,500 = ₹15,000 sitting on top of whatever else you do.

**Why this and not "passive repos":** there is no real way to earn without either (a) skill you already have, (b) effort, or (c) money. This is the lowest-effort option that still works: the template is done, so your effort is only sales messages. Everything else that promised "no effort" money is a scam or a trap.
