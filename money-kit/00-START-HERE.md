
# START HERE — Your 30-day plan to your first ₹10,000

Realistic promise: **first paying client in 7–21 days, ₹2,000–₹8,000 in month 1** if you do the daily tasks. No coding required. No investment required (except ~₹0 — even hosting is free).

Time needed: **2–3 hours a day for the first 3 days, then 45–60 minutes a day** (mostly sending WhatsApp messages).

---

## Day 1 — Set up everything (≈3 hours)

### 1.1 Get your links live (30 min)
1. Create a **GitHub account** → github.com/signup (free).
2. Create a new repository: name it `mysite`, set to **Public**, click Create.
3. Open the repo → **Add file → Upload files** → drag in these two files from `github-repo/`:
   - `index.html`
   - `demo-cafe.html`
4. Settings → Pages → *Source:* Deploy from branch → Branch: `main`, folder `/ (root)` → Save.
5. Wait 2 minutes. Your site is now live at: `https://YOURUSERNAME.github.io/mysite/`
6. Open `index.html` from the repo, click the pencil ✏️, and replace the 4 placeholders described at the top of the file:
   `[YOUR NAME]`, `[YOUR CITY]`, `91XXXXXXXXXX` (your WhatsApp number, digits only with 91), `hello@youremail.com`.
7. Optional but strong: buy a short domain like `yourname.in` (~₹300–600/year on Hostinger/GoDaddy) and point it to the Pages site. Not required — the github.io link works fine.

✅ **Checkpoint:** open your link on your phone. It should look good and the WhatsApp button should open a chat with you.

### 1.2 Money rails (20 min)
- UPI: your bank UPI ID is enough. Also create a **RupeeInvoice / Vyapar / Zoho Invoice** free account so you can send a PDF invoice (clients trust invoices; it makes the work look professional). Details in `sales/payment-setup.md`.
- Ask 3 friends: "if I send you ₹1, can you send it back on this UPI?" — confirms it works and shows you the credit SMS.

### 1.3 Create up reselling accounts (30 min)
- **Fiverr** and **Upwork** accounts (free). Paste the listings from `sales/upwork-fiverr-listings.md` — both are prepared word-for-word.
- **Google Maps** list: search `cafe near me`, `salon near me`, `gym near me` in Patna. Save **50 businesses** into a Google Sheet: name, phone/WhatsApp, rating, area. These are your clients. Businesses with a website ⇒ skip (they already understand). No website but active reviews ⇒ perfect target.

### 1.4 Build your demo links (30 min)
For each business type, the same template with swapped text becomes a "free sample" you can send as bait:
- Café → `demo-cafe.html` (already done)
- Salon → copy `demo-cafe.html` → rename `demo-salon.html`, change name/tagline/menu→services/prices
- Gym → `demo-gym.html`, same idea
Use `client-template/customize.md` — it walks through exactly which lines to change. 10 minutes per demo.

## Day 2 — Start conversations (≈90 min)

Send **30 WhatsApp messages** using the Hindi or English scripts in `sales/pitch-scripts.md`. Rules that make this work:

- Message from **your personal number**, with a photo profile and your name. Not a business account.
- Your openers must include **one specific detail about their business** ("aapke cafe ke reviews 4.7 hain") — copy-paste blasts get ignored and can get your number flagged.
- Wait for interest before sending the demo link. Sending a link first feels like spam.
- **Free first draft** is your superpower. "Pehla draft free hai, pasand aaye tohi pay karein" converts because there's zero risk.
- Send between **11 AM–1 PM and 4–8 PM** — when shop owners are free.

**The math:** 30 messages → ~8 replies → ~2–3 interested → ~1 free draft → ~0.5 client per day of messaging. 3 days of messaging = your first client. That's just statistics, not talent.

Also that day: post your new site link on WhatsApp status + Instagram story: "I build one-page websites for local businesses — first draft free. DM me." (2 minutes, surprisingly effective)

## Day 3 — Close your first client

When someone says "kitna lagega" (how much), don't negotiate down. Say:

> "Sir, launch price ₹1,999 hai, aur pehla draft free. Aapko pasand aaye to 50% advance, site live hone ke baad 50%. Guarantee: nahi pasand to kuch nahi dena."

Then:
1. Collect content: business name, services + prices (4–8 items), timings, address, phone, 3–5 photos, logo or a colour they like. **Template for collecting this** is in `client-template/customize.md`.
2. Build with the client template: 30–60 minutes.
3. Send the draft link. Get the 50% UPI payment. Make the changes. Deliver.
4. Hosting: their own free GitHub Pages account (do it with them) or your account with a folder per client. For paid custom domain, client pays you ₹500–800/yr for the domain — buy it under their name.

**Delivery message** (Hindi):
> "Site live hai! 🎉 Link: [link]. 7 din tak koi bhi fix free hai. Aapke jaan-pehchan mein 2 business hain jo website chahte hain? Referral par aapka next update free. 🙏"

Referrals are how this becomes real income — every happy café owner knows 10 other shop owners.

## Weeks 2–4 — Scale

- Keep sending 20–30 outreach messages daily (`sales/daily-playbook.md` has the exact routine).
- Reply within 1 hour on Fiverr (response time affects ranking).
- Post one "before/after" or finished-site screenshot on Instagram/WhatsApp status weekly. Ask each client: "May I put this on my portfolio?" Most say yes.
- Raise prices: after client #3, Starter → ₹1,499. After client #10, drop Starter entirely and start at ₹1,999.
- After 10 clients and ~₹15,000, add **₹500/month maintenance** to every new client (updates + backup). 20 clients × ₹500 = ₹10,000/month recurring.

## What to NOT do (saves you months)

| Temptation | Reality |
|---|---|
| "Create repo, earn passive money" videos | No platform pays for repos. Scams and courses. |
| Buying ₹X "website builder reseller" license | Your free template already beats these. |
| Freelancing without a portfolio | That's why we build the demo first. Portfolio first = trust. |
| Spamming 500 people daily | Number gets banned in 2 days. 20–30 personalised beats 500 blasts. |
| Lowering price below ₹999 | ₹199 clients demand the most work. Cheap attracts the worst clients. |
