
# The daily playbook — 45 minutes, every day, for 30 days

Print this. Tick boxes. The only thing that kills this business is stopping before message #200.

## Morning (20 min) — Find & message

- [ ] Open your Google Sheet of businesses (50+ targets, add 10 new every day)
- [ ] Pick **10 businesses** whose last contact was 3+ days ago or never contacted
- [ ] For each: open Google Maps → note rating & area → send the personalised WhatsApp script (`pitch-scripts.md`)
- [ ] Log: name | date | reply yes/no | next follow-up

## Midday (10 min) — Marketplaces

- [ ] **Upwork:** 3 proposals on jobs posted in the last hour
- [ ] **Fiverr:** answer all messages (speed = ranking)
- [ ] Update your "response time" by checking the app twice more in the day

## Evening (15 min) — Close & build

- [ ] Reply to every interested lead **the same day** (send demo link or free draft plan)
- [ ] Send free draft within 24 h of any "yes" — speed is your entire competitive advantage
- [ ] One piece of content: WhatsApp status with a site screenshot ("Client #X live in 24 hours ✅")
- [ ] Ask every delivered client for a referral + Google review of *you*

## Weekly (Saturday, 30 min)

- [ ] Review the sheet: who replied, who ghosted, who bought
- [ ] Follow up #5 (`pitch-scripts.md`) with anyone who replied but didn't buy
- [ ] Raise prices? (after every 3rd client, +₹500)
- [ ] Add one new demo variant (salon / gym / clinic) using `client-template/`

## Expected numbers (just run the stats)

| Metric | Rate | In 30 days of 10 messages/day (300 msgs) |
|---|---|---|
| Replies | ~25% | ~75 |
| Interested | ~30% of replies | ~22 |
| Free drafts built | ~50% of interested | ~11 |
| **Paying clients** | ~40% of drafts | **~4–5 clients** |
| **Month-1 revenue** | avg ₹1,500 | **₹6,000–₹8,000** |

Add Fiverr/Upwork on top: 1–2 orders/month early on (₹2,000–₹5,000) → **total ~₹8,000–₹13,000 in month 1 is a normal outcome, not luck.**

## The two rules that decide everything

1. **Speed.** Reply within 1 hour. Deliver drafts in 24 h. A slow freelancer loses to a fast average one, every time.
2. **Volume with personalisation.** 10 personalised messages/day beats 100 blasts. Your number must stay unbanned — that's your whole business line.

## When you have 5 testimonials / 10 clients

- Move to **₹1,999 minimum** and stop doing ₹999 work entirely.
- Launch the **₹500/month maintenance** subscription to past clients ("updates, backups, priority WhatsApp support"). 20 clients = ₹10,000/month.
- Hire a friend to do outreach on 30% commission while you build.
- Keep the portfolio growing: every client gets a 1-line case study ("Salon in Kankarbagh went live in 24 h; 12 new WhatsApp enquiries in week 1").
