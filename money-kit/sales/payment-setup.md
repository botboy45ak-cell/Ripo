
# Money setup — get paid cleanly from day one

## 1. UPI (your main rail)

- Your bank's UPI ID (e.g. `name@okhdfcbank`) is enough. No company needed.
- Test it before your first client: ask a friend to send ₹1 with the note "website test".
- **Always collect 50% advance.** It filters out time-wasters completely. A client who won't pay ₹499 advance was never going to pay ₹999.
- Keep a second UPI app installed (GPay + PhonePe/Paytm) — some clients' banks fail on one app.

## 2. Invoices (5 min setup, makes you look 10× bigger)

Free options:
- **RupeeInvoice / Refrens** (Indian, free tier, GST-style invoices) — recommended
- **Zoho Invoice** (free plan, PDF + email)
- **Vyapar** app (Hindi-friendly, popular with small businesses)

Invoice must contain:
```
INVOICE #2026-001        Date: DD/MM/YYYY
From: [Your Name], Patna, [Phone], UPI: [UPI ID]
To: [Business Name], [Address]
Item: One-page website design & development  ₹1,999
Paid (advance): ₹999 | Due: ₹1,000
Payment via UPI: [UPI ID] / QR
Note: Balance payable on go-live. 7 days free fixes from delivery.
```

**If your annual turnover is under ₹20 lakh, you generally don't need GST registration** — but check with a CA if clients ask for GST invoices. Simple rule for now: don't promise GST invoices; most local clients don't ask.

## 3. A tiny agreement (paste into WhatsApp, get a "Yes")

Even a WhatsApp "ok" forms a contract in India. Send this before starting:

```
Project: One-page website for [Business]
Includes: [1 page / mobile friendly / WhatsApp button / Maps / free hosting setup / 2 rounds of changes]
Price: ₹[amount] (50% advance ₹[x] now, 50% on go-live)
Delivery: draft within 24 h of content received
Support: 7 days free fixes after go-live
Excludes: domain cost (₹[500–800]/yr, paid by client), extra pages (₹300/page), content writing (₹200/page)
Note: website content, photos & business details are provided by the client
Please reply "Yes, start" to confirm. 🙏
```

Save that text as a WhatsApp quick-reply / keyboard shortcut. Never start work without a "yes".

## 4. Hosting & delivery

| What | Cost | How |
|---|---|---|
| Hosting (GitHub Pages) | **Free, forever** | Client's own GitHub account, or a folder/repo under yours |
| Repo for client's code | Free (public) | You can also sell "source code handover" as a premium add-on |
| Domain (optional) | ₹500–800/yr | Buy **in the client's name** (their PAN/email) — you charge them cost + setup. Never buy domains for clients in your own name |
| SSL | Free | GitHub Pages does HTTPS automatically |
| Backups | Free | Keep a copy in your Google Drive, one folder per client |

**When a client wants WordPress instead:** don't. Out of scope — either learn it later and quote separately, or decline politely ("main custom coded sites karta hoon, WordPress alag service hai").

## 5. Pricing hygiene

- Quote in a message, not verbally — screenshot becomes the record.
- **Never** start with discounts. If they push: "Launch price already hai. Maintenance add karein to ₹500/month, kabhi bhi cancel."
- Payment terms on every invoice: "50% advance, 50% at go-live."
- Chase a late final payment with a friendly calendar reminder at +3 days, then a firm one at +7 ("site live hai, balance ₹X baaki hai — aaj kar dijiye please 🙏"). Your site can't be "repossessed," so the 50% advance is your protection.
- Keep records: a simple Google Sheet (client, amount, advance, balance, date) is enough until ₹1 lakh/year.

## 6. Tax (keep it simple, don't ignore it)

- Freelance income is taxable under "Income from Profession (44ADA presumptive)" — you can declare 50% of receipts as profit. If income stays under the basic exemption (₹3–4 lakh new regime), you likely owe zero tax — but **file an ITR anyway** (freelancers need it for loans/visas later).
- Keep one bank account or clearly separate ledger for this income.
- The moment monthly income crosses ₹50k consistently, spend ₹1,500 on a CA consultation. Not before.
