
# Pitch scripts — copy, personalise, send

**Rule #1:** never send a link in the first message. **Rule #2:** always include one real detail about their business (rating, a dish, their area). **Rule #3:** message between 11 AM–1 PM or 4–8 PM.

---

## 1. WhatsApp opener — café / restaurant (Hindi)

```
Namaste [Name] ji 🙏
Main [Your Name], Patna se. Aapka [Cafe Name] Google pe dekha — 4.7 rating, bahut badhiya! 👏

Ek cheez notice ki: online koi website nahi hai jahan customers menu, timings aur location ek jagah dekh sakein.

Main local businesses ke liye ek-page website banata hoon — 24 ghante mein ready, WhatsApp button ke saath (customer seedha aapko message kar sake).

Pehla draft bilkul FREE hai — pasand aaye tabhi pay karna. Bhej doon?
```

## 2. WhatsApp opener — generic (salon / gym / boutique / coaching)

```
Namaste [Name] ji 🙏
Main [Your Name], Patna se — websites banata hoon local businesses ke liye.

[Business Name] ke baare mein online dekha — [specific detail, e.g. "aapki salon Patna ki sabse purani hai" / "aapke gym ke reviews shandar hain"].

Aapke customers roz Google pe "salon near me" / "gym near me" search karte hain. Website hone se wo aap tak seedha WhatsApp pe pahunch sakte hain.

Mera offer simple hai: pehla draft free, 24 ghante mein. Pasand na aaye to kuch nahi dena. Bhej doon?
```

## 3. WhatsApp opener (English)

```
Hi [Name], I'm [Your Name] from Patna — I build one-page websites for local businesses.

I found [Business Name] on Google ([specific detail — rating/area]). You have great reviews, but no website — so customers who search online can't easily reach you.

I can build you a clean mobile-friendly site with your services, prices, timings and a WhatsApp button — live in 24 hours, starting at ₹999. The first draft is free to see, you pay only if you like it.

Want me to send a free draft?
```

## 4. When they reply "kitna / how much"

```
Launch offer chal raha hai:
• Starter — ₹999 (1 page, 24 hrs)
• Business — ₹1,999 (3 pages, Maps + social links) ← most clients
• Premium — ₹3,499 (5 pages, gallery, 30-day support)

Pehla draft free. Pasand aaye → 50% advance, site live hone ke baad 50%.
Guarantee: draft pasand na aaye to ek rupaya nahi. 🙏
```

## 5. Follow-up (send after 24h silence, max 2 total)

```
[Name] ji, bas ek chhota reminder 🙏 Draft free hai — banane mein aapka kuch nahi jaata.
Nahi chahiye to koi baat nahi, main dubara message nahi karunga. 🙏
```

Second follow-up after 3 more days (only if they replied once): send the demo link directly as the final nudge.

```
Socha aapka bhi aisa dikh sakta hai ⬆️ 24 ghante mein, aapka content, aapka number.
Interested ho to bata dijiye, warna no problem — all the best! 🙏
```

## 6. Phone call script (30 seconds, when WhatsApp is ignored)

> "Namaste [Name] ji, main [Your Name] bol raha hoon, Patna se. Main local businesses ke liye websites banata hoon. Aapke [business] ke liye ek sample dekhna chahte the — bilkul free hai, 24 ghante mein bana ke bhej doon? Bas 2 minute ka kaam hai aapke liye."

If "not interested": "Bilkul theek hai sir, yeh mera WhatsApp number hai — kabhi bhi zaroorat ho to message kar dijiye. Dhanyavaad! 🙏" (End with dignity; these calls recycle months later.)

## 7. In-person pitch (walk into the shop, best conversion rate)

> "Sir, main [Name], websites banata hoon chhote businesses ke liye. Aapke shop ke liye ek free sample bana ke dikha sakta hoon — 24 ghante mein. Pasand aaye to hi baat aage badhaenge. Aapka naam aur services bata dijiye, kal dikha dunga."

Bring your phone, show the demo café site. Leave a slip with your number. **This works absurdly well in India — Indian shop owners respect in-person effort far more than unknown WhatsApp messages.**

## 8. Objection handling

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "Zaroorat nahi hai" | "Bilkul samajhta hoon. Ek free draft dekh lijiye bas — pasand na aaye to delete kar dijiye. Risk zero hai." |
| "Pehle se Instagram hai" | "Instagram bahut achha hai! Website Google search mein aati hai — jab koi 'salon near me' search karta hai to Instagram nahi dikhta. Dono saath best chalte hain." |
| "Bahut mehnga hai" | "₹999 ek baar ka hai — ek din ki chai sales se bhi kam. Isme 7 din free support bhi hai. Aur aap selfie pe ₹2,000 kharach kar dete hain 😄" |
| "Sochta hoon / baad mein" | "Theek hai ji, lekin offer launch pricing hai — pehle 5 clients ke baad ₹1,999 se shuru hoga. Aaj confirm karein to ₹999 lock ho jayegi." |
| "Pehle dekh ke batata hoon" (wants to see first) | "Bilkul — wahi to plan hai. Main aaj hi free draft bana deta hoon. Bas naam aur services bata dijiye." |
| "Aap kaun ho? Trust nahi" | "Sahi baat hai sir. Mera portfolio: [link] — live site khol ke dekh lijiye. Payment bhi site live hone ke baad hi." |

## 9. Referral ask (send after they say "nice work")

```
Bahut khushi hui aapka site pasand aaya! 🙏
Ek chhoti si request: aapke jaan-pehchan mein 2 business hain jinko website chahiye?
Unke liye ₹300 discount, aur aapke liye next update free. Patna mein sab ek dusre ko jaante hain 😄
```

## 10. Where to find targets (daily, 10 min)

1. Google Maps → search "cafe Patna", "salon Patna", "gym Patna", "coaching Patna", "boutique Patna", "dentist Patna", "cloud kitchen Patna" → businesses with **rating ≥ 4.0** and **no website link** → these are hot.
2. Instagram local hashtags: `#patnacafe #patnasalon #patnafood` → DM.
3. Justdial / IndiaMART listings without websites.
4. WhatsApp groups: your colony group, college group, society groups → "sabko pata hai main websites banata hoon, free first draft, forward karna chahein to 🙏"
5. Your phone's contact list: every shop you've ever bought from.
