I build exactly these: [1 sentence matching their ask]. Here's a live example in the same style: [YOUR DEMO LINK]

For your project I'd:
1. [specific step matching their requirement]
2. [specific step]
3. Deliver in [X] days with [what they asked for] included

Fixed price: $[amount]. I'm available to start today.

One question to make sure I understand: [smart specific question — this is what wins replies]

**The Upwork rule:** apply within 30 minutes of a job posting, keep proposals under 150 words, and always ask one smart question. Response rate triples.
