CUEFRAME PROTOTYPE — DRAFT USE TERMS

This is a proposed plain-language license for review only. It is not legal advice and is not an approved commercial license. Do not publish or sell the prototype until the seller's guardian and, where appropriate, a qualified adviser review the terms and the marketplace rules.

If a final license is adopted, the buyer would receive a non-exclusive, non-transferable permission to place the included artwork in the buyer's own finished videos, including videos that are monetized or made for a client. The buyer may edit the artwork for use inside a finished video.

The buyer would not be permitted to resell, share, upload, sublicense, or distribute the original PNG/SVG files (or substantially unchanged variants) as standalone assets, templates, overlays, or another asset pack. The buyer would not claim ownership of the raw artwork or use it as a logo/trademark. No ownership of third-party editing apps, marks, or footage is included.

A final seller must specify refund, support, usage, jurisdiction, and other terms consistent with the chosen platform and applicable law. This draft does not create a buyer/seller agreement until validly offered and accepted through an eligible seller account.
