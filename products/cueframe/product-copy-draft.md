# Draft listing copy — not for publication yet

## Working title

Cueframe — Cross-Device Short-Form Visual Cue Kit

## One-line description

A compact set of original transparent PNG cues for adding clearer steps, emphasis, comparisons, and recaps to short-form videos on phone or desktop editors.

## What the buyer would receive

- 16 visual cue types in portrait 9:16 and landscape 16:9 formats.
- Three visual styles: Clear, Pulse, and Warm.
- Transparent PNGs for direct image-layer import, plus optional editable SVG source.
- Phone, desktop, and general import guides.
- Local preview gallery and examples for tutorials, product demos, comparisons, and personal-story videos.
- An offline, responsive storyboard planner for four formats (how-to, demo, comparison, story) that exports a time-coded plain-text plan.

## What it does not include

No native project templates, animated transitions, music, footage, fonts, plugins, personalized editing, or one-to-one support. The PNGs are static; buyers add their own text in their preferred editor. No editor compatibility has been fully tested yet.

## Draft audience

Creators making tutorials, explainers, product demonstrations, lists, gaming/tech clips, and short personal stories who want reusable visual structure without app-specific presets.

## Claims to avoid

Do not claim guaranteed reach, viral results, higher retention, time savings for every editor, universal compatibility, exclusivity, or validated sales. Do not claim affiliation with CapCut, Adobe, Blackmagic, or any social platform.

No price, refund policy, or marketplace has been selected. Revisit this copy after device tests, market validation, and seller/tax checks.
