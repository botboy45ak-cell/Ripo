# Cueframe market check — 26 November 2026

## Decision

Build only a small prototype for review and a real phone/desktop compatibility test. Do **not** claim the product is validated, choose a sales price, open a seller account, or publish a checkout based on this evidence alone.

## Direct listing observations

- **Jasper Cullen, Short Form Essentials Overlay Pack** — fetched 26 Nov 2026. The page showed £4.97, 5 MP4 overlay files, 40 sales, and 1 rating. It does not specifically promise CapCut Mobile support. This is a small, adjacent paid signal for short-form video overlays, not proof of demand for Cueframe. [Listing](https://jaspercullen.gumroad.com/l/overlaypack)
- **MotionVault Reel Pack** — fetched 26 Nov 2026. The page showed a $0+ price, 26 vertical MP4 overlays/transitions, compatibility claims for desktop and mobile editors, 55 downloads, and a 254.4 MB ZIP. Downloads at a zero-dollar minimum are not paid sales. [Listing](https://motionvault.gumroad.com/l/vsmbsa)
- Earlier research in this project also found a mobile-compatible 120-transition pack with a $0 minimum and 893 downloads. That is again a download count, not confirmed paid purchases. The product's actual audience and conversion rate are unknown.

## Workflow feasibility

CapCut's official overlay guide, dated 21 October 2026, includes workflows for PC, web, and phone. It describes importing a video/image overlay and adjusting its position and blending. Cueframe uses ordinary transparent PNG layers, aiming to avoid dependence on desktop-only presets, plugins, proprietary project files, or blend modes. [CapCut guide](https://www.capcut.com/resource/free-video-overlay-effects)

## What this does not prove

- There is no reliable public dataset proving demand for this exact text-free cue kit, for a new seller, or in a particular country.
- Existing listings' cumulative counts do not reveal recent sales velocity, profit, conversion rate, buyer device, or the creator's traffic source.
- The user has not confirmed a niche, audience, or content channel. The three styles are product design hypotheses, not evidence that these niches will buy.
- Account age, guardian ownership, payout/KYC, Indian GST registration, income tax, and platform terms are separate questions. Nothing here says the route is tax-free or guarantees that a platform is usable.

## Validation gate

1. Test portrait PNG import and export on the user's phone and CapCut Mobile.
2. Test one wide PNG in a desktop editor (a borrowed or free editor is fine; do not spend money).
3. Collect written feedback only if the user chooses to; do not cold-message or DM people.
4. Recheck current platform, guardian/KYC, payment, fee, GSTIN, and income-tax requirements before opening a store.
5. Continue only if the assets are useful in real edits and a legitimate zero-upfront-cost selling route is confirmed.
