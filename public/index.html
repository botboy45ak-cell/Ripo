Common questions

A few useful answers.

Check each tool's instructions for the formats and options available on that page.

Are HandyKit's tools free to use?

Yes. The tools are free to use and do not require an account.

Are my files uploaded?

The tools are designed to process files in your browser. Read the note on each tool page; background removal may download a supporting model on first use.

Does HandyKit provide official photo or signature requirements?

No. Enter the current dimensions and size limit stated by the relevant form or organization.

Will image compression always reach an exact KB size?

No. It tries to stay under your chosen maximum, but the result depends on the source image and settings. Review the output.
