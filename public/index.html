Good to know

Clear answers before we start.

Will this guarantee more students?

No. It only helps present class information and organize replies/follow-ups. Admissions depend on many things outside this setup.

Do I have to give you my passwords?

No. You keep control of your accounts. The setup is designed for written instructions and files you control; no account password is needed.

Do we need a call or meeting?

No live call or in-person meeting is included. Scope, review and handover are intended to be handled in writing.

Where is student information stored?

This public site does not collect or store student enquiries. The tracker is a template for your own account. Only enter information you need, and protect access to your file.

Will you send WhatsApp messages to parents?

No. The reply templates are for you to review and send. Only message people who have asked to hear from you or given permission, and respect opt-outs.

Can you promise first-page Google ranking?

No. Nobody should promise that. This pilot does not include SEO or ad management.
