A simple workflow

Browse. Shape. Use your own story.

These tools are starting points for creators—not a promise of a finished video, universal editor support, or a commercial product.

01

Browse a visual cue

Choose Clear, Pulse, or Warm and find a portrait or landscape PNG/SVG that fits the moment.

02

Make motion your own

Build a cue in Motion Studio or start with a sample; preview your exact export in the editor you use.

03

Plan the edit

Use the offline storyboard planner to organize shots, narration, and timing before you edit.
