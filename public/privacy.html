Privacy for enquiry forms

This page is a small service enquiry site. It does not use advertising trackers, analytics cookies, or a student-management database.

What the form collects

If you submit the enquiry form, it collects the name and email address you enter, what you teach, an optional area, and your consent to receive a reply. Please do not include student records, health information, passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information.

Why and where it is processed

The information is used only to respond to your setup request. The form is processed by Netlify and submissions are stored in the site's Netlify dashboard. The site owner may enable email notifications so they can reply. Netlify handles the hosting and form processing under its own terms and privacy practices.

Retention and requests

The site owner should delete enquiry records when they are no longer needed. To ask about a submission or request deletion, contact the owner through the linked Instagram profile.

Instagram

If you choose the Instagram link, Instagram's own service and privacy terms apply. The site does not send a DM for you.
