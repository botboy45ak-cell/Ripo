# Cueframe Motion Studio sample exports

These short clips were exported from the current Motion Studio prototype in Chromium. They demonstrate animated overlay graphics on a solid green-key background; they are **not finished videos** and contain no source footage or audio.

| File | Preset | Canvas | Tempo | Playback duration |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `step-reveal-portrait.mp4` | Step reveal | 540×960 portrait | 120 BPM | ~1.90 s |
| `orbit-loop-square.mp4` | Orbit loop | 540×540 square | 90 BPM | ~2.65 s |

The clips were opened in Chromium to verify that each MP4 has readable video metadata and a non-zero duration. Import/codec compatibility in other browsers, phones, or editing apps is not guaranteed. Use chroma key to remove the green background if your editor supports it.
