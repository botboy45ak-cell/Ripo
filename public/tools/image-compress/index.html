Helpful answers

Questions about Compress image

Can I compress an image to 50 KB?

Enter 50 as the maximum size in KB. The tool tries to meet that limit, but results depend on the image, format, quality, and dimensions.

Will compression change image dimensions?

If adjusting quality is not enough to meet the limit, the tool may also reduce the dimensions. Check the output size and appearance.

Which formats can I save?

The compressor offers JPG and WebP output. Compare quality before sharing or uploading the result.
