Privacy for enquiry forms

This page is a small service enquiry site. It does not use advertising trackers, analytics cookies, or a student-management database.

What the form collects

If you submit the enquiry form, it collects the name and email address you enter, what you teach, an optional area, your consent, and an optional UPI transaction reference if you choose to provide one. Do not enter your UPI PIN, OTP, passwords, bank details, student records, or other sensitive information.

Why and where it is processed

The information is used to respond to your setup request and, if you provide one, manually match a UPI payment reference. The form is processed by Netlify and submissions are stored in the site's Netlify dashboard. Email notifications require separate setup by the site owner. Netlify handles the hosting and form processing under its own terms and privacy practices.

UPI payments

Payments made by scanning the QR or opening the UPI link are handled by FamApp and your chosen UPI app, not by this website. The site does not automatically verify payment. Check the payee name in your payment app before confirming, and keep your transaction reference.

Retention and requests

The site owner should delete enquiry records when they are no longer needed. To ask about a submission or request deletion, contact the owner through the linked Instagram profile.

Instagram

If you choose the Instagram link, Instagram's own service and privacy terms apply. The site does not send a DM for you.
